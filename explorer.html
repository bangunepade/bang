Explorer guide

How to verify

  • Copy the full transaction ID from your receipt.
  • Paste it into the search field and press Search.
  • Review type, asset, amount, status, and full date/time/year.
  • Download CSV to analyze the currently filtered public records.

Public data & privacy

Public records omit account UIDs and private wallet balances. Searching an internal wallet address can show matching activity recorded for that address. Do not publish secrets or credentials.

Ledger disclaimer
These are internal platform records. A completed status does not independently prove a blockchain transfer or guarantee redeemable value.
