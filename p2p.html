How internal escrow works

  1. For a SELL offer, the seller's selected asset is locked when the offer is published. For a BUY offer, the seller accepts and the full required asset amount is locked atomically from the seller's internal wallet into escrow.
  2. The circular purple-and-green seal appears only after the backend confirms the internal ledger lock. The buyer then completes the agreed external payment separately.
  3. Buyer marks payment as sent; seller must verify receipt independently before releasing BNG.
  4. Seller releases the selected asset to the buyer, or either party can open a dispute. Disputed funds remain locked until an authorized administrator resolves them.

Internal escrow does not send on-chain tokens, does not verify M-Pesa/bank payments, and does not provide a blockchain wallet. Do not release an asset until payment is actually received.
