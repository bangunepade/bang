# BANG BNG P2P — Global payment method catalogue

The P2P offer form now lists 183 distinct payment-method options in 8 groups:
- Bank and card
- Mobile money
- Online payment services
- Other
- Instant bank and local transfer networks
- Card networks
- Digital wallets and regional apps
- Cash, vouchers and local collection

The picker uses searchable, selectable cards with colour-coded monogram marks. Country defaults suggest a relevant subset; traders can search and select other listed methods. The Netlify function allow-list has been expanded to match the UI values.

Important: these are payment methods traders may arrange directly between themselves. Listing a method does not mean BANG BNG processes payments, has a provider integration, verifies the payment, or guarantees that the method is available in every country. Traders should only select methods they can receive and independently verify payment before releasing escrow assets. The monogram marks are locally rendered brand-style identifiers, not official provider logo files.
