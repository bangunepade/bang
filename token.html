How internal BNG works

Internal BNG balances, transfers, mining records, and swaps are maintained by the website ledger. They are not an on-chain token balance and do not represent a deposit at a public blockchain address.

Use the wallet, mining, and swap pages to view and manage supported internal ledger records. A displayed balance or reference price is not a guarantee of cash value or redemption.
