Official website information

What is BANG BNG?

BANG BNG is a community-oriented digital-asset and Web3 project presented through the 1bang.top website. The site provides project information and a set of platform features, including account access, internal wallet and balance views, mining and staking interfaces, swap tools, P2P marketplace features, an explorer-style activity view, market information and community support.

The project developer is identified on the website as BANGUNE. Project support contact: support@1bang.top.

Important distinction: internal records are not on-chain assets

Some BNG balances, mining rewards, staking records, transfers, swaps and P2P escrow activity shown by the website are maintained as internal platform ledger records. A displayed balance or transaction record is not, by itself, proof of an on-chain token, blockchain transaction, external-wallet asset, bank payment or cash value.

The project notes describe mining rewards as provisional display records rather than on-chain BNG. Internal swaps and escrow are platform-ledger operations, not blockchain transactions. The P2P service does not itself hold or automatically verify external M-Pesa or bank payments.

BNG price information

The BNG value displayed on the site is labelled an internal reference price, not a live exchange quote. It should not be interpreted as a verified market price, guaranteed redemption value, proof of liquidity, or a promise that BNG can be sold at that price.

Other cryptocurrency market prices shown on the website are informational market-data displays and may be delayed or unavailable. Displaying a cryptocurrency logo or price does not mean that deposits, withdrawals, swaps or P2P trades for that asset are available.

Platform features and limitations

  • Wallet: provides account-based balance and wallet interfaces. Internal balances should not be confused with externally controlled blockchain wallets.
  • Mining: displays platform mining activity and provisional internal reward records; these are not automatically on-chain mining rewards.
  • Staking: displays platform staking features and records. Users should review the current terms and risks shown in the application before participating. Returns are not guaranteed by this information page.
  • Swap: internal swaps depend on configured rates, account balances and available platform reserves. They are not necessarily trades on an external exchange or blockchain.
  • P2P: the platform describes an internal-ledger escrow workflow. External payment transfers between users are not automatically held or independently verified by the website.
  • Explorer: an internal activity view is not proof that an event was recorded on a public blockchain.

BANG BNG Layer 1 network status

The website's Developer Hub lists November 2026 as a target for the BANG BNG Layer 1 network. This is a target, not confirmation that the network has launched. Network availability, chain configuration, RPC details, external-wallet compatibility and transfer functionality must be independently confirmed through official project announcements before users attempt a transfer.

Wallet logos displayed in the Developer Hub identify wallets being evaluated for possible compatibility; their presence is not proof that those wallets currently support BANG BNG L1.
