# BNG-only mining and BNG-paired swaps

- Only BNG accrues as a mining reward (internal website ledger).
- Other supported assets are available through swaps with BNG as one side.
- Direct token-to-token swaps are rejected by the server; swap back to BNG first.
- Swap requests still require configured server-side rates and sufficient treasury reserves.
- These are internal ledger entries, not proof-of-work mining, on-chain assets, reserves, or guaranteed payouts.
- Existing historical non-BNG balances are preserved; they are not newly mined.
- P2P escrow remains BNG-only.
