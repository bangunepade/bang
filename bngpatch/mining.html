Mining rewards are internal website ledger entries. Only BNG mining is displayed. Rewards remain internal ledger entries, not on-chain assets or guaranteed payouts. Mining should only be enabled against a verified deposited internal mining-contract balance; this ZIP does not yet establish the source field for that balance.

Why are these balances shown as internal?

They are website ledger entries. The page does not perform proof-of-work mining or automatically create real blockchain assets. Swaps require configured conversion rates and treasury reserves. P2P escrow is currently BNG-only.

How do swaps and P2P work?

Internal swaps only complete when server-side rates and treasury reserves are configured. P2P offers currently support BNG only; other assets must not be offered through escrow until multi-asset escrow settlement is implemented and tested.
