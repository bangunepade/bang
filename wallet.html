Your internal BNG address

This automatically generated address belongs to your 1bang.top account. Share it only with people sending you internal BNG. It is not a blockchain deposit address and cannot receive on-chain coins.

Decorative wallet artwork. Use the live address shown below and the Copy my address button; do not copy an address printed inside the artwork.

Send internal assets

Send BNG, BTC, BNB, or USDT ledger balances to another registered internal wallet address.

Minimum BNG transfer: 10 BNG. Other asset transfers keep their existing amount rules. A 0.50% transfer fee is charged in BNG and credited to the configured admin account. For BTC, BNB and USDT, the fee requires server-configured BNG conversion rates. This is not a blockchain gas fee.

No email OTP is required. Confirm each transfer carefully; completed ledger transfers may not be reversible.

Transfers are internal ledger entries. These generated addresses cannot receive native BTC, BNB, or USDT on public networks.

Administrator balance controls

Admin-only. Credits create internal ledger balances and do not create or prove real BTC, BNB, USDT or BNG reserves. Every action is audit logged.
